Price data transparency

Prices come from an Agmarknet snapshot ending around March 2026, for potato, onion and tomato only. These are historical records, not today's live prices. Availability varies by mandi.

PotatoOnionTomato

From your voice to a useful answer

  1. Speak naturallyTap the mic and ask for a crop and a mandi. You can also ask for yesterday's record.
  2. Speech becomes textGnani Prisma transcribes the recording. The app is tuned for Punjabi and Punjabi-English mixed speech.
  3. Intent is checkedRule-based matching finds the crop, mandi and day. When details are missing or unclear, the assistant asks instead of silently guessing.
  4. The reply is spokenA price is retrieved from the local Agmarknet snapshot, placed into a fixed Punjabi reply and spoken using Gnani Timbre.
